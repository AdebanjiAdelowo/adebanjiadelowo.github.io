Multimodal RAG · Document understanding

Multimodal Visual RAG ↗

Question answering over document pages containing text, tables, charts and images.

Compares OCR-based keyword and dense retrieval with image embeddings, hybrid fusion and reranking; evaluates answer grounding, page citations and abstention. On the documented textbook corpus, text hybrid retrieval was a strong baseline and visual retrieval did not show a meaningful improvement. GPU experiments remain separately pending.

Legal RAG · Citation verification

Legal Research RAG ↗

Source-grounded case-law retrieval and research summaries with citation checks.

Evaluates BM25, dense and hybrid retrieval, reranking, jurisdiction and date constraints, citation support and abstention. On 2,000 test queries, BM25 reached nDCG@10 of 0.176; normalized-score hybrid reached 0.186. Citation existence checks worked reliably, but substantive support and abstention remain limitations.

Research implementation · not legal advice; no citator
