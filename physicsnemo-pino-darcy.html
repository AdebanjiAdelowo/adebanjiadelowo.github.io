PINO Darcy

I investigated when adding a physically consistent PDE residual to a Fourier Neural Operator improves Darcy-flow pressure predictions—and when a lower physics residual does not translate into better accuracy.

Status: Research release v1.0.0 published (November 2026). MPS experiments, primary CUDA replication and G8 regularisation controls are complete. This is a versioned research/software release, not a peer-reviewed paper or independent GPU reproduction.

Research question and governing equation

Darcy flow describes pressure-driven movement through a porous medium. The problem is to learn a mapping from spatially varying permeability a(x) to pressure u(x), governed by the elliptic PDE −∇·(a(x)∇u(x)) = f(x), where f(x) is the forcing term.

I studied NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo’s physics-informed Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) example to answer two questions: How does physics-loss weighting change prediction error and PDE residual? Under what training and numerical conditions does physics-informed training outperform an otherwise comparable data-only FNO?

What I investigated

  • Physical and numerical consistency: checked the residual forcing and finite-difference grid against the normalized reference data.
  • Physics-loss weighting: compared the upstream residual, a corrected data-consistent residual and a shared data-only baseline across multiple weights.
  • Training budget and seeds: distinguished early 15-epoch findings from 50-epoch studies with five paired seeds in the primary comparison.
  • Residual discretisation and labels: compared four residual formulations and examined 25, 50 and 102 labeled examples, including training-only unlabeled permeability fields.
  • Hardware replication: repeated the primary comparison on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 CUDA GPU after Apple MPS experiments.
  • Conventional regularisation: compared the corrected PINO model with weight decay and validation-best checkpoint selection in the G8 follow-up.

Important finding: the upstream physics residual was inconsistent

The normalized reference data satisfy a PDE with forcing approximately 57.2, but the original upstream training residual used approximately 0.0175—effectively the reciprocal. Consequently, reducing that original residual was not equivalent to matching the PDE represented by the dataset.

I evaluated the residual as shipped alongside a corrected, data-consistent definition that adjusts the forcing and grid spacing. Even the corrected cell-grid residual is only an approximation to the reference discretisation, particularly near sharp permeability changes.

In G8, the paired mean absolute difference was −0.0081651 (95% paired Student-t interval: −0.0088331 to −0.0074972). The exact two-sided sign-test p-value was 0.0625. Five favorable pairs are informative, but this small sample and the previously examined test split limit inferential claims.

Key interpretation: reducing a PDE residual and improving pressure-prediction accuracy are different objectives. The training budget, physical consistency, residual weight and discretisation all mattered.

Methods, implementation and reproducibility

The implementation uses Python, PyTorch, NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo, Fourier Neural Operators, numerical finite-difference residuals, Apple MPS and NVIDIA CUDA. The repository adds controlled experiment configurations, metrics, validation scripts, tests, result records and a scientific audit around the upstream model and training implementation; the underlying FNO architecture and training loop originate with NVIDIA.

The release includes tracked source code, configurations, recorded results and documentation. The dataset must be obtained separately, and some full checkpoints and external experiment archives are not distributed in the repository. Passing offline CI tests does not independently reproduce all GPU training runs.

For a detailed record, consult the research README, scientific audit, reproducibility checklist and G8 archive verification.

Limitations and future directions

  • The results concern one Darcy-flow dataset, its particular train/validation/test split and one main FNO architecture; cross-dataset or cross-PDE generalisation has not been established.
  • Near permeability discontinuities, the corrected finite-difference residual remains an imperfect proxy for the reference operator.
  • Five-seed comparisons have limited statistical power. The G8 test split had been examined previously, and historical provenance metadata require careful interpretation.
  • The primary comparison was replicated on CUDA, but the full weight, label and discretisation sweeps were not independently reproduced on that hardware.
  • Useful next investigations include genuinely external holdouts, additional PDEs and architectures, and independently rerunning archived experiments with complete checkpoints.
