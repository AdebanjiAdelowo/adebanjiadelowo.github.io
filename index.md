# Adebanji Oluwatimileyin Adelowo

**Applied Mathematician · Scientific Machine Learning Researcher · Machine Learning Engineer**

I work at the intersection of numerical mathematics, scientific computing, machine learning, and engineering. My interests include numerical partial differential equations, inverse problems, dynamical systems, data assimilation, and reliable machine-learning models for scientific applications.

## Featured projects

### [Finite-element simulation of flow around a cylinder](https://github.com/AdebanjiAdelowo/fem-cylinder-flow)
Numerical simulation and analysis of benchmark incompressible flows, including drag, lift, and vortex shedding.

### [Darcy inverse problem](https://github.com/AdebanjiAdelowo/darcy-inverse-problem)
Finite-element modeling and inverse reconstruction of spatially varying coefficients, with numerical verification.

### [Lorenz-96 data assimilation](https://github.com/AdebanjiAdelowo/lorenz96-data-assimilation)
Chaotic dynamical systems and ensemble-based state estimation.

### [Neural surrogate modeling for Burgers' equation](https://github.com/AdebanjiAdelowo/neural-surrogate-burgers)
A comparison of reduced-order and learned surrogate approaches for a nonlinear PDE.
