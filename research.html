Research

My research connects mathematical modelling, scientific machine learning and numerical verification.

Scientific machine learning

I compare reduced models and learned operators against numerical reference solutions, separating accuracy on familiar data from generalisation to new initial conditions.

  • Reduced models and neural operators for Burgers’ equation

Inverse problems and uncertainty

I study learned reconstruction under model mismatch and distribution shift, and ensemble filtering for chaotic dynamics.

  • Photoacoustic reconstruction
  • Lorenz-96 data assimilation

Numerical PDEs and fluid dynamics

My work includes finite-element cylinder-flow benchmarks and constrained passive-scalar mixing. Analytical references and convergence studies help distinguish numerical effects from physical conclusions.

  • Finite-element cylinder-flow benchmarks
  • MSc mixing research and later numerical study
