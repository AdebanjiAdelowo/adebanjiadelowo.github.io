I am an applied mathematician and machine-learning engineer with a background in numerical analysis, scientific computing, and data-driven modeling.

I earned an MSc in Mathematical Engineering from the University of L'Aquila and a BSc in Mathematics from Obafemi Awolowo University.

My work combines mathematical modeling, numerical algorithms, Python-based scientific computing, and applied machine learning. I am particularly interested in methods that remain interpretable, verifiable, and useful for physical and engineering systems.
