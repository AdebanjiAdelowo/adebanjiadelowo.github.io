# About

I am an applied mathematician and machine learning engineer working across numerical analysis, scientific computing, inverse problems, and applied AI.

I earned an **MSc in Mathematical Engineering** from the University of L’Aquila, Italy, and a **BSc in Mathematics (First Class Honours)** from Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria.

My mathematical background includes numerical PDEs and computational methods. My professional experience includes aviation telemetry, signal processing, machine-learning systems, ETL pipelines, and software services.

I am especially interested in methods whose predictions can be evaluated against physical models and reproducible numerical experiments.
