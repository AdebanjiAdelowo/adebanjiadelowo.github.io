I am testing whether physics-informed fine-tuning improves a graph model of Stokes flow, and which errors it might make worse.

Status: The reduced-study results have been supplemented by a 1,000-geometry audit and recovery tests. Pressure generalisation remains unresolved; this is not a full-budget benchmark.

Experimental setup

Reduced local study with paired evaluation on two test meshes; 4,000 baseline iterations and 10,000 physics-stage iterations.

Interpretation and scope

On the two meshes in the original comparison, fine-tuning improved velocity and lowered momentum and divergence residuals, while the boundary residual increased. Subsequent geometry validation and recovery tests strengthened the evaluation checks, but pressure overfitting remains an important limitation.

Next steps

Investigate pressure overfitting, boundary trade-offs and performance across broader held-out geometries.
