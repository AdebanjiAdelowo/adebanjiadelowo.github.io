Scientific machine learning

I am interested in when a smaller, faster model can stand in for a full numerical solver. My Burgers’ equation study compares reduced models and neural operators, including what happens when the initial conditions change.

Inverse problems and uncertainty

I look at what happens when a model faces data that differ from what it was trained on. That question appears in my photoacoustic reconstruction work and in my experiments with filtering chaotic systems.

  • Photoacoustic reconstruction
  • Lorenz-96 data assimilation

Numerical PDEs and fluid dynamics

I use numerical methods to study fluid-flow problems, including flow past a cylinder and the mixing of passive scalars. Checking convergence and comparing with reference solutions matter to me because a convincing plot is not enough to establish accuracy.
