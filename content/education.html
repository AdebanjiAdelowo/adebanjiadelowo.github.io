M.Sc. Mathematical Engineering

2018 to 2021 · University of L'Aquila, Italy

  • For my master’s thesis on passive-scalar mixing, I studied theoretical bounds on how quickly incompressible flows mix a scalar. I also simulated an optimal-stirring strategy with a pseudo-spectral solver. The work drew on results by Iyer, Kiselev and Xu, and by Lin, Thiffeault and Doering. Supervisor: Prof. Stefano Spirito.
  • Coursework: PDEs, Functional Analysis, Numerical Analysis, Numerical Methods for PDEs, Galerkin / Finite Element Methods, Scientific Computing, Dynamical Systems and Bifurcation Theory, Advanced Optimisation, Machine Learning, Fluid Dynamics
