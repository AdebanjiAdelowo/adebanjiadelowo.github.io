I am looking at whether deeper message passing helps a graph model predict vortex shedding over long time periods.

Status: This study is still in progress. The results below come from the smaller experiments described here, not the full-budget upstream setup.

Experimental setup

Nine models: three depths × three seeds, 11,960 gradient steps each on Apple MPS.

Interpretation and scope

Deeper models made better one-step predictions, but none of the tested rollouts kept the vortex shedding going past step 100.

Next steps

Investigate autoregressive stability and evaluate a longer training budget.
