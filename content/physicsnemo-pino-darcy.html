I am testing whether adding a physics-based loss helps a neural operator predict Darcy flow more accurately.

Status: A v1.0.0 research release is available. The expanded MPS and CUDA replication studies are complete; regularisation and broader generalisation questions remain.

Experimental setup

The initial three-seed, 15-epoch comparison was followed by a 63-run MPS study and a separate T4 CUDA replication against a data-only comparison. The findings apply to those experimental configurations.

Interpretation and scope

The initial small comparison did not show a consistent prediction-accuracy benefit. The later, larger study and CUDA replication did show lower prediction error against their data-only controls. This does not establish the same improvement for other datasets, training budgets or architectures.

Next steps

Complete regularisation controls and evaluate generalisation outside the reported settings. The v1.0.0 research release records the published study.

The repository includes the configuration, scripts, saved results and audit notes if you want to check how I ran the experiments.
