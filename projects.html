Reduced models & neural operators

How well can a smaller or learned model reproduce a nonlinear PDE solution?

I compared classical reduced models with neural surrogates for Burgers’ equation. Across 40 in-distribution cases, POD reached 0.152% relative L² error, compared with 0.90% for FNO (five seeds).

Dynamical systems · Uncertainty

Data assimilation for chaotic Lorenz-96

Can we recover the state of a chaotic system when the observations are noisy and incomplete?

I tested an Ensemble Kalman filter on Lorenz-96. With the selected localisation radius, the RMSE fell from 4.63 to 0.41 across 10 seeds; the unlocalised filter had diverged.

Multimodal RAG · Document understanding

Multimodal Visual RAG

Can a question-answering system find useful evidence across pages that mix text, charts, tables and images?

I compared text-based retrieval with visual and hybrid approaches, then checked how well the answers were grounded in the source pages. On the textbook corpus I tested, the text-hybrid baseline was strong; adding visual retrieval did not give a meaningful improvement. The GPU experiments are still pending.

Legal RAG · Citation verification

Legal Research RAG

I wanted to see how reliably a retrieval system could find case law and support its summaries with citations.

I tested keyword, dense and hybrid retrieval, along with reranking, jurisdiction and date filters, citation checks and abstention. Across 2,000 queries, BM25 scored 0.176 nDCG@10, while normalized-score hybrid retrieval reached 0.186. Checking whether a citation exists worked well; checking whether it truly supports a claim and knowing when to abstain were harder.
